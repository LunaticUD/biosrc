`fasterq-dump SRR28119110`

`fasterq-dump SRR28119110`
# 下载完成后进行压缩文件，节省硬盘空间
```bash
cat *.1 > SRR28119110_1.fastq
cat *.2 > SRR28119110_2.fastq
gzip SRR28119110_1.fastq
gzip SRR28119110_2.fastq
```
